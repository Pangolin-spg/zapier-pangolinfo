## Recommended Zap Template titles

1. Track Amazon competitor prices and stock in Google Sheets with Pangolinfo.
2. Send new low-star Amazon reviews to Slack for complaint triage.
3. Monitor Amazon organic and Sponsored keyword rankings in Google Sheets.
4. Build a weekly Amazon niche opportunity shortlist in Airtable.

AI Overview and Amazon Alexa are not offered to new Zapier users because their synchronous response times can meet or exceed Zapier's 30-second action limit. AI Overview remains hidden in v1.0.3 solely for migration compatibility with existing Zaps. Use the direct [AI Overview SERP API](https://www.pangolinfo.com/ai-overview-serp-api/), [AI Overview documentation](https://docs.pangolinfo.com/en-api-reference/aiModeSerpApi/aiModeSerpAPI), [Amazon Alexa API](https://www.pangolinfo.com/amazon-alexa-api/), [Alexa documentation](https://docs.pangolinfo.com/en-api-reference/amazonAlexaAPI/amazonAlexaAPI), n8n, Make, Apify, or MCP until asynchronous Zapier-compatible flows are available.

Each template should explain its business outcome, setup, credit use, data freshness, error behavior, and the most relevant Pangolinfo product/documentation links. Do not repeat an identical block of promotional copy across templates.
